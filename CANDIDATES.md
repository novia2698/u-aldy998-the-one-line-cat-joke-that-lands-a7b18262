# Joke candidates — The One-Line Cat Joke That Lands

The judge (z-ai/glm-5.3-flash, pairwise) rewards, in order: a one-line cat
joke with a setup and a turn that lands; laugh-out-loud on first read;
surprise, misdirection or a fresh angle over a heard-it-before pun; no
padding; retellable to a friend the same day. Winner takes the whole $2.

## Shortlist

1. (146 chars, PICKED) "My cat knocked my coffee off the table, held eye
   contact the whole way down, and purred — the world's tiniest criminal
   with absolutely no regrets."
2. (118 chars) "I bought my cat a luxury bed; she sleeps in the box it came
   in, on top of the receipt, judging my financial decisions."
3. (93 chars) "My cat stares at a blank wall for hours, and after my rent
   went up, I finally understand her."
4. (113 chars) "I told my cat she's adopted; she looked at me like she'd
   known all along and was just waiting for me to catch up."
5. (101 chars) "My cat only sits on my keyboard when I'm working, which is
   how I found out she's actually my manager."

## Why #1 wins the shortlist

- Setup and turn: an everyday accident (knocked coffee) flips into a
  deliberate crime the moment the cat holds eye contact and purrs.
- Fresh angle: the cat is not cute or aloof, it is a smug little criminal
  who knows it will get away with it — a character, not a pun.
- Visual and retellable: one image (eye contact all the way down) carries
  the whole joke, so it survives being told to a friend the same day.
- The others lose on first read: #2 leans on the worn-out box joke, #3 is
  a smile rather than a laugh, #4 needs a beat to land, #5 is a familiar
  work-from-home gag with "manager" doing all the work.

Tie-break note: an exact tie on judge win rate goes to the smaller text
(fewer bytes), then to the earlier receipt — another reason to keep the
winner short and submit early rather than polishing it longer.
